# LumaTrace brand assets

LumaTrace is the sole product name. Publisher: Clover F. The visual identity uses royal blue and white.

- `AppIcon.icns`: macOS application icon.
- `BrandMark.png`: standalone brand mark.
- `en.lproj`, `es.lproj`, `zh-Hans.lproj`: localized macOS display names. The product name remains **LumaTrace** in every locale.

The symbol combines overlapping photo frames, a subtle hourglass-like negative space, and a small AI sparkle. Keep the icon clear at small sizes; do not add translated product names or third-party marks.
